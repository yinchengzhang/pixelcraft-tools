Why PixelCraft?

Files stay on your device during image processing. No account, no tracking of your images, and no hidden watermarks. Compression uses browser image encoding, so the requested size cannot always be achieved without reducing dimensions or quality.

HEIC support uses an optional conversion library loaded from a public CDN; your selected photo is processed locally in your browser. Large files may take longer on mobile devices.
